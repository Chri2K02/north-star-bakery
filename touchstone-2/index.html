Welcome to North Star Bakery

Fresh bread, flaky pastries, and celebration cakes, baked by hand every morning right here in the neighborhood. Whether you are grabbing a croissant on the way to work or ordering a cake for a family celebration, there is always a warm seat and a fresh loaf waiting for you.

What We're Known For

Our neighbors know us for slow-rising sourdough, buttery croissants, and custom cakes made for birthdays, weddings, and office parties. Everything is mixed, shaped, and baked in small batches in our own kitchen.

Featured Item of the Week

This week, try our cinnamon swirl buns, rolled with brown sugar and cinnamon and finished with a vanilla glaze. They are available every morning until they sell out, or you can pre-order a dozen for pickup.
