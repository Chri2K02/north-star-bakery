Our Products

Everything on our shelves is baked fresh each morning. Prices below are general ranges. To reserve an item or ask about a custom order, please use our pre-order request form.

Breads

  • Signature Sourdough Loaf: Our most popular bread, with a chewy crumb and crisp crust.
  • Whole Wheat Sandwich Bread: Soft and hearty, perfect for school lunches.
  • Rosemary Focaccia: Topped with olive oil, sea salt, and fresh rosemary.

Price range: $6 to $12 per loaf

Pastries

  • Butter Croissants: Flaky, golden, and made with real butter.
  • Seasonal Fruit Scones: Made with local fruit when it is in season.
  • Cinnamon Swirl Buns: Finished with a vanilla glaze.

Price range: $3 to $6 each, or $30 to $55 per dozen

Cakes

  • Celebration Cakes: Custom layer cakes for birthdays, showers, and anniversaries.
  • Event and Office Cakes: Sheet cakes for meetings, parties, and community events.
  • Mini Cakes: Single-serving cakes for small celebrations.

Price range: $8 for mini cakes to $90 for large custom cakes

Custom cakes need at least 48 hours' notice. Please mention any allergies in your order request.
